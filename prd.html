01Context

Foodeez is a multi-cuisine restaurant on New College Road, Taunsa Sharif — Pakistani, Chinese, BBQ and fast food under one roof, with dine-in, takeaway and home delivery. Today orders arrive over phone calls, WhatsApp and Facebook DMs. That works at 20 orders a night; it breaks at 80.

The problem

Orders get lost between calls and chats. Prices quoted differ from the menu card. No record of who ordered what, and no way to know when a rider will arrive.

The opportunity

Taunsa has no restaurant with its own ordering site. Foodeez's social following (TikTok, Facebook) can be converted directly into orders without paying aggregator commission.

Brand

"Taste the joy." Loud yellow, heavy black, a cheeky chef with a moustache. Confident, local, fun — never corporate. ذائقہ خوشی کا

05Order lifecycle

One status model drives the customer tracker, the counter board and the kitchen display.

NewPlaced by customer. Counter has 2 min SLA to accept. Audible alert.
PreparingAccepted; ticket on KDS. Timer starts.
ReadyKitchen bumped. Pickup: customer notified. Delivery: assign rider.
Out for deliveryRider assigned and left. Customer sees rider name & phone.
DeliveredCash collected / paid. Order closed, counts to revenue.

Any non-final order can be Cancelled with a reason. Pickup orders skip "Out for delivery" (Ready → Delivered = collected). Tickets older than 20 min in Preparing turn red on the KDS.

06Admin panel

Dashboard

Today's revenue, orders, average order value, avg prep time — each vs same day last week. 7-day revenue chart, top-selling items, live order queue, store open/closed switch.

Orders

Kanban by status with one-tap advance; list view with search/filter; order detail with customer, items, payment, rider assignment, print receipt, cancel.

Kitchen display

Dark, high-contrast tickets in order of age with timers and modifiers in bold. Single "Bump" action. Designed for a wall-mounted tablet.

Menu

Table of all items by category: image, price, tags, availability toggle (86 an item instantly), edit drawer for name/desc/price/category/tags, add new item.

Deals & promo codes

Create percentage or flat codes with minimum order; activate/deactivate; usage count.

Customers

Auto-built from orders: name, phone, order count, lifetime spend, last order. Identify regulars.

Riders

Roster with on-shift toggle, current delivery, deliveries today, cash to collect.

Reservations & Settings

Table bookings to confirm. Store hours, delivery fee, free-delivery threshold, min order, ETA, contact numbers.

09Non-functional

Performance

LCP < 2.5s on 3G. Hero video ≤ 1.3MB, poster frame first, images lazy-loaded WebP.

Reliability

If WebSocket drops, tracker polls every 15s. New-order alerts repeat until acknowledged.

Accessibility

44px tap targets, AA contrast (black on yellow passes at 15:1), reduced-motion disables video.
